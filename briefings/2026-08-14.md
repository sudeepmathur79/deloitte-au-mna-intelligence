# Deloitte Australia M&A Intelligence — 14 August 2026

## Executive briefing

- **EQT / Cleanaway is the standout Australian M&A development:** EQT Infrastructure has proposed a **A$3.13/share** acquisition of Cleanaway, valuing equity at roughly **A$7bn** and enterprise value at **A$9.4bn**. Cleanaway has granted up to nine weeks' exclusive due diligence and its board currently intends to recommend the proposal, subject to final review. The offer represents a **32.1% premium** to the prior close. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/commentary/breakingviews/eqt-unearths-7-bln-aussie-trash-to-cash-deal-2026-08-13/)
- **The deal thesis is infrastructure + operational value creation:** Cleanaway is Australia's largest waste/recycling services provider; EQT has global environmental-services experience and the target's FY26 guidance implies underlying EBIT of A$480–500m. The transaction is therefore a useful case study in infrastructure-style capital buying a listed operating platform where network density, pricing, fleet efficiency and waste-to-energy/circular-economy optionality matter. [Cleanaway](https://www.cleanaway.com.au/newsroom/1hfy26-results)
- **FleetPartners is becoming a contested takeover auction:** Orix has entered at A$3.80/share (~A$820m), alongside PEP at A$4.00/share (~A$852m) and Element's earlier proposal. A possible Smartgroup bid is also being discussed. The situation is particularly interesting because PEP owns SG Fleet, creating potential competition/regulatory complexity around fleet management. [The Australian](https://www.theaustralian.com.au/business/dataroom/japans-orix-comes-out-of-left-field-to-join-fleetpartners-takeover-battle/news-story/b594d9bc7a437d0baedc9002e7e6b7ca)
- **Healthscope remains a live stressed-sale situation:** a Calvary-led consortium including PEP, Healthe Care, Acurio Health and KnG Group has increased its offer, but the decision remains delayed amid unresolved issues. The proposed break-up allocates hospitals across multiple operators, making separation, TSA, clinical operating-model and transition execution central to value preservation. [The Australian](https://www.theaustralian.com.au/business/dataroom/calvaryled-bid-for-healthscope-faces-delay-amid-unresolved-issues/news-story/b8f34a84f1d3291c8f7d33c7b46ae02)
- **Inghams is a watch-list PE target:** TPG Capital, BGH Capital, PEP, Paine Schwartz and Roc Partners are reportedly evaluating the poultry producer; international strategic interest has also been reported. No formal bid is expected before Inghams' FY26 results on 21 August. [The Australian](https://www.theaustralian.com.au/business/dataroom/private-equity-firm-puts-inghams-on-the-menu/news-story/a11f922d1309fa67f9bfd3834112a6a2)

## Major transactions / live situations

### EQT → Cleanaway

- Buyer: EQT Infrastructure
- Target: Cleanaway Waste Management
- Indicative price: A$3.13/share
- Equity value: ~A$7bn
- Enterprise value: ~A$9.4bn
- Premium: 32.1%
- Status: exclusive due diligence for up to nine weeks; board currently intends to recommend.

**Deloitte lens:** buy-side commercial/operational DD, infrastructure value-creation plan, network/fleet optimisation, carve-out/integration if required, technology-enabled field operations, HSE transformation, procurement and post-deal operating model.

### FleetPartners auction

- Orix: A$3.80/share (~A$820m)
- PEP: A$4.00/share (~A$852m)
- Element: competing proposal
- Possible Smartgroup interest

**Deloitte lens:** contested auction support, synergy modelling, regulatory/competition assessment, integration planning and operating-model/value-creation work. PEP's ownership of SG Fleet makes portfolio/competition analysis especially relevant.

### Healthscope

Calvary-led consortium has increased its offer; the proposed structure would split hospitals among Calvary, Healthe Care, Acurio Health and KnG Group. The alternative is a not-for-profit 'PurposeCo' structure. Receivers represent lenders owed roughly A$1.6–1.8bn.

**Deloitte lens:** stressed M&A, separation/carve-out, TSA design, operational continuity, technology separation, workforce transition and value preservation under compressed timelines.

## Corporate M&A

The strongest corporate signal is the continued willingness of strategic buyers and sponsors to pay for **scarce operating capability and infrastructure platforms**, not merely earnings scale. Cleanaway's network of collection vehicles, facilities, transfer stations, landfills and treatment assets gives the asset a strategic infrastructure profile.

## PE

PE is active across both large-cap take-privates and complex situations. Cleanaway and FleetPartners show two different strategies: infrastructure capital buying a scaled essential-services platform, and sponsor/strategic competition for a smaller listed platform with consolidation optionality. Inghams shows continued interest in public companies where operational underperformance may create a buy-and-fix thesis.

## Infrastructure / institutional / sovereign capital

Cleanaway is the clearest current signal. Essential-services infrastructure remains attractive where assets have high barriers to entry, contracted or recurring demand, network density and operational improvement opportunities. Waste also offers a long-term circular-economy and energy-transition angle.

## Cross-border

## ACCC / FIRB / regulation

The new Australian merger-control regime is now a central diligence consideration. ACCC guidance confirms that notifications can become stale after 12 months if an acquisition is not put into effect, and the ACCC can use compulsory information-gathering powers during assessment. For serial acquisitions and contested takeovers, competition analysis should therefore be built into the transaction strategy early rather than treated as a late legal workstream. [ACCC Merger Reform FAQ](https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/merger-reform-frequently-asked-questions-24-april-2026.pdf)

## Deloitte AD lens

1. **Infrastructure capital is moving into operational transformation territory.** The differentiator is no longer just capital availability; it is the ability to articulate and execute EBITDA improvement.
2. **Carve-outs remain a major opportunity.** Healthscope and Austal show how complex separation can become the critical determinant of deal value.
3. **Contested auctions require an integrated commercial + operational + regulatory story.** FleetPartners is a good example.
4. **Stressed M&A creates compressed transformation opportunities.** Healthscope requires value preservation while ownership and operating models change simultaneously.
5. **PE target origination should look beyond obvious sector multiples.** Inghams illustrates how temporary operational/industry pressure can create a sponsor entry point.

## My right-to-play

The strongest positioning is **technology-enabled transaction execution and post-deal value realisation**, rather than presenting as a pure M&A banker. Relevant angles include technology DD, separation architecture, TSA design, target operating model, cloud/data transformation, integration architecture, enterprise transformation and value-realisation governance.
